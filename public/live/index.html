Request to share an application window

Join the classroom waiting list. After your lecturer approves you, choose one application window (such as Visual Studio). Only one student can share at a time. No recording.

Only application windows are permitted—not the full monitor or a browser tab. On standby, your window stays captured locally (the browser sharing indicator remains on), but its video upload to LiveKit is paused. Your lecturer can resume it without another browser prompt. Stop sharing, close the application or leave the classroom to end capture.

Your lecturer must approve you before an application can be shared. Browser permission is requested only after approval.

Ready when you are.
