# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

CIA4103-LabBoard is an educational, non-commercial project for collaborative learning, sharing code, and giving constructive feedback. We welcome people of all backgrounds and skill levels, and we expect a respectful, inclusive, harassment-free environment.

## Expected behavior

- Be courteous and patient when discussing questions, errors, and code.
- Give specific, constructive feedback; focus on the work, not the person.
- Respect different experience levels, perspectives, and accessibility needs.
- Obtain permission before sharing someone else's code, image, or screen.
- Protect privacy: do not post credentials, personal data, confidential student information, or screenshots containing private material.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insults, trolling, repeated disruptive posting, or deliberately misleading others.
- Unwanted disclosure of another person's identity or private information.
- Sharing offensive or inappropriate material through code, images, comments, or screen sharing.
- Misusing the classroom board to disrupt teaching or access another person's work without consent.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to contributions and interactions in this repository, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related classroom collaboration. It also applies when representing the project in public.

## Reporting concerns

For a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainer **privately** using a contact method listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/NTinkicht). If a private contact method is unavailable, open a brief repository issue **requesting a private reporting channel**, without including personal information or sensitive incident details.

Reports will be considered fairly, with discretion and respect for the privacy of those involved. Do not use public issues to disclose another person's personal or sensitive information.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer may remove inappropriate content, request changes, warn participants, restrict contributions, or block access when necessary. Responses will reflect the seriousness and recurrence of the behavior. Good-faith reports will not be punished.

## Educational use

This project supports learning and demonstration. Its discussion board is public when deployed without access controls; users should avoid submitting identifiable or sensitive information. Participation in screen sharing must always be voluntary.
